## Install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/thekirankumarv/Vaultly-releases/releases/latest) on your Android phone.
2. Download `Vaultly-vX.Y.Z.apk` from **Assets**.
3. Open it. If Android asks, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file manager.
4. Open Vaultly and follow the one-minute setup: a backup passphrase and where to back up.

Needs Android 7.0 or newer on a 64-bit (arm64) phone, which covers almost every phone from the last several years.

## Features

### 💳 Pay cards
- Add a credit, debit or prepaid card by photographing the front and back, or picking the photos from your gallery
- Bank, card number, expiry, name, network and CVV are **read on your phone** and never uploaded; you check everything before saving
- Shown as a virtual card: 12 designs in glass, matte or metal finish. The photos aren't kept
- Card number and CVV are encrypted; showing or copying them asks for your fingerprint or screen lock
- **Share** card details with the number masked or in full, never the CVV
- Screenshots are blocked while card details are on screen

### 🗂️ Wallet
- All your cards stacked with every bank visible; pull down to fan them out
- Tap a card to bring it forward, swipe sideways to flip it, swipe down to put it back
- One-tap copy for number, name, expiry and CVV

### 🪪 ID cards
- Driving licence, RC, PAN, Aadhaar, voter ID, memberships and more, with the front and back photos
- Every labelled detail is read from both sides: name, ID number, date of birth, address, blood group, validity and more; keep or discard each one
- Flip the card, open the photo full size, copy any detail with a tap
- **Press and hold** an ID card in the list for a quick big preview of both sides
- ID numbers are encrypted and hidden until you tap the eye

### 📄 Documents
- Scan with auto-crop and enhance (several pages at once), pick photos, or upload a PDF
- PDFs show their first page; multi-page scans swipe page by page
- **Press and hold a page** for a round magnifier that follows your finger, so fine print is easy to read (photos and PDFs)
- **Share or save as PDF or as images**, whatever format you added it in: both sides go in one share, and saves go straight to Downloads
- Categories (Insurance, Property, Education, Bills…), validity dates and **expiring soon** on Home

### ✅ Review before saving
- Tick the details to keep, untick the rest, drag to reorder, edit anything
- Mark any detail as secret so it stays hidden until revealed
- Leaving the screen asks first, so nothing you scanned is lost by accident

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Family
- Add parents, partner and kids, each with their own cards and documents
- Kept separate from yours everywhere; pick who an item belongs to when adding it

### 📤 Send to another Vaultly user
- Send an ID card, document or pay card to someone far away: **⋯ → Send to Vaultly user**, and the share sheet opens with the `.vaultly` file and a short message with the code and a link to get Vaultly, ready for WhatsApp, Telegram, email or Quick Share
- They tap the file, open it with Vaultly and add it in one tap: to themselves, a family member, or a new person such as a friend
- **Nothing is lost:** original images and PDFs, every detail and the validity date arrive exactly as saved
- **Auto-delete:** never, 1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or a date you pick. A glowing countdown shows on the item; when time runs out it's removed from their phone, even if it comes back from a backup
- Protected by an 8-character code (or no code, your choice). Send it in the same message, or untick that and share it yourself. Pay cards go with the last 4 digits unless you choose the full number
- **Share history** in Settings keeps the codes you sent for 24 hours (hidden until you tap), so you can copy one again
- Timed items are view only: saving, sharing, copying and screenshots are all turned off

### ✨ Ask Vaultly
- Tap the glowing orb and ask in plain words: *"mom's Aadhaar number"*, *"Nova credit card"*, *"what expires soon"*, *"list all documents"*
- Answers come as cards you can open, copy or share; tap ↻ to start a fresh chat
- Worked out **on your phone**; your numbers and documents are never sent anywhere

### 💾 Backup
- **Encrypted with your passphrase**, set once at the start; you only type it again when restoring
- **Automatic backup** to a folder you pick, after every change: the latest copy plus one per day for a week, including your document files. The folder stays when the app is uninstalled
- **Google Drive (optional):** a private, encrypted copy with all files in a hidden Vaultly folder in your own Drive
- **Android's Google backup** also keeps your vault data
- **Export** a backup file any time; **restore** on a new phone from the folder, Drive or a file

### 🔒 And more
- **App lock** with fingerprint, face or your screen lock
- Light, dark or system theme
- **Help & FAQ** built in, and **Send feedback** from Settings
- **In-app updates:** a popup when a new version is out, checked before it installs

## Smarter document reading (optional)

Pay cards and ID cards are always read on your phone. For other documents you can add Google Gemini with **your own free API key** for smarter reading. Everything works without it.

1. Open [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey) and sign in with your Google account.
2. Tap **Create API key** and copy it.
3. In Vaultly go to **Settings → Gemini API key**, paste the key and tap **Check & save**.

**Private AI** is on by default: your phone reads the text first and replaces every name, number and date before anything is sent, so Gemini only sees the layout. The key is stored encrypted on your phone and is never part of backups.

## Privacy

- Everything lives **on your phone**. There's no account, no server and no analytics.
- Card numbers, CVVs and ID numbers are encrypted (AES-256) and need your fingerprint or screen lock to show or copy.
- Pay cards and ID cards are read on your phone; their photos and details are never uploaded.
- Gemini is used only if you add your own key, and with Private AI on it never sees your real names, numbers or dates.
- Ask Vaultly answers on your phone; your values are never sent anywhere.
- Backups are encrypted with your passphrase and go only where you choose. Google Drive backup uses a hidden Vaultly folder the app can't see beyond.
- Full [privacy policy](https://getvaultly.vercel.app/privacy/).

## Updates

Vaultly checks this page for new versions and shows a popup with what's new. Tap **Update now**, or update later from **Settings → App updates**. Your data stays as it is. Every version is also listed under [Releases](https://github.com/thekirankumarv/Vaultly-releases/releases).

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Is it really free?</b></summary>

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases. If you'd like to say thanks, there's a **Support the developer** option in Settings.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why isn't it on the Play Store?</b></summary>

It's distributed here on GitHub, so Android asks once for permission to install it. In-app updates are downloaded from this page and their checksum is verified before installing.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What if I forget my backup passphrase?</b></summary>

Nobody can recover it, including us, because your backups are encrypted with it. Write it down somewhere safe when you set it. Your vault on the phone keeps working; only restoring a backup needs it.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I move to a new phone?</b></summary>

Keep a copy off the old phone: connect **Google Drive** in **Settings → Backup**, or copy your backup folder somewhere safe. On the new phone install Vaultly, tap **Restore from a backup**, pick the folder, Drive or file, and enter your passphrase. Cards, documents, family and settings all come back.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Will I lose my data if I uninstall?</b></summary>

Not if a backup folder is chosen. Vaultly saves to it after every change, and the folder stays when the app is removed. Reinstall, tap **Restore from a backup** and pick the same folder.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Are my card photos kept?</b></summary>

No. Pay card photos are only used to read the details and are then deleted; the wallet shows a virtual card. ID card and document photos are kept on your phone (and in your backups) so you can open and share them.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I keep my family's cards too?</b></summary>

Yes. Add members in the **Family** tab. Their cards and documents stay separate from yours, and you can ask for them by name or relation: *"dad's PAN"*.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I send a document to someone else who uses Vaultly?</b></summary>

Yes. Open it, tap **⋯ → Send to Vaultly user**, choose when it should auto-delete and whether it needs a code, and tap **Create and send**: the file and a short message with the code go out together on any chat app. Lost the code? **Settings → Share history** keeps it for 24 hours. They open it with Vaultly (or **Settings → Receive shared item**), enter the code and pick who it belongs to. Images, PDFs and every detail arrive exactly as you saved them. There's no server, so a sent item can't be taken back, so choose an auto-delete time instead.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I lock the app?</b></summary>

Yes. **Settings → App lock** asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock when Vaultly opens and after you've been away for more than a minute. It needs a screen lock set up on your phone.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it work offline?</b></summary>

Everything except Google Drive backup, Gemini reading and update checks works with no internet at all, including card and ID reading.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Found a bug or have an idea?</b></summary>

Use **Settings → Send feedback** in the app, or open an [issue](https://github.com/thekirankumarv/Vaultly-releases/issues).
</details>
